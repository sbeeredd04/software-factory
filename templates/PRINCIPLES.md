# Project principles

Draft each module separately: product, conventions, risk, evals. Reference those
files from configuration; they remain the source of authority.

## Intent and outcomes

State the user problem, success conditions and non-goals.

## Decisions and authority

Reference approved scope, material-risk escalation and external-write decisions.
Record unknowns as questions; a generated draft is not approval.


## Consequences and reservations

Assess cause, affected behavior/data/files/environments/tenants/trust/money,
method and actual permissions, effect proof, reversibility and blast radius.
Reuse owner autonomy and scoped GO for routine work. Reassess at every phase;
unknown authority, material tradeoffs, new trust scope, irreversible effects and
explicit reservations need the applicable owner decision. Record proceed, deeper
review or escalation with rationale and blocked dependents; continue independent
approved work unless the owner selected stop-and-ask. Technical topic alone does
not determine whether another human decision is needed. Preserve existing policy
until the owner explicitly amends it; quality and evidence floors always apply.
