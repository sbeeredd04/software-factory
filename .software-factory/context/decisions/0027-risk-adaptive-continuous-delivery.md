# 0027 — Continuous delivery with risk-adaptive human decisions

Status: accepted · 2026-10-15

## Context

The whole software factory ships continuously. Human involvement depends on
the work, affected assets, risk and requested autonomy. Per-epic stops misstate it.
BMad skills and their structured epics and stories are central to planning.

## Drivers
Continuous delivery, consequence-based escalation and explicit user autonomy.

## Options
Mandatory per-epic stops; or conditional human decisions with protected evidence.

## Outcome

The host owns discovery, prioritization, BMad epics/stories, staffing, build,
independent review, proof, integration, delivery and authorized observation.
BMad uses its separate pinned installer; never vendor it or simulate execution.

Assess consequences against scope, autonomy, risk principles and host permissions.
Escalate material risk, unclear authority, tradeoffs and owner-reserved decisions when
needed. Routine authorized work continues without repeated prompts. Human
escalation is a conditional branch at any phase, not an obligatory epic terminus.

Keep evidence and independent review. Reserved human checkpoints remain binding;
judgment cannot manufacture approval, expand permissions or erase a decision.
Delegated acceptance is explicit, evidence-bound and distinct from human acceptance.
Preserve legacy configurations and history without silently broadening authority.

## Consequences

Supersede unconditional per-epic stops, preserving proof and external authority.
Update skills, onboarding, mechanics, examples and visuals together.

## Confirmation
Verify actual setup and BMad availability, regression tests and independent review.
This product amendment grants no release, deployment or monitoring access.
