# 0027 — Continuous delivery with risk-adaptive human decisions

Status: accepted · 2026-10-15

## Context

The owner clarified that the product is a whole software factory that ships
continuously. Human involvement depends on the work, affected assets, risk and
requested autonomy. A mandatory human stop after every epic misstates that goal.
BMad skills and their structured epics and stories are central to planning.

## Outcome

The host orchestrator owns discovery, prioritization, BMad planning, staffing,
implementation, independent review and proof, integration, delivery verification,
and authorized observation feeding the next iteration. BMad is installed
separately through the approved pinned installer; its content is never vendored.
Unavailable planning capabilities remain explicit, never simulated execution.

Assess consequences throughout the cycle against the owner's scope, autonomy
preferences, risk principles and actual host permissions. Escalate material risk,
unclear authority, consequential tradeoffs and owner-reserved decisions when
needed. Routine authorized work continues without repeated prompts. Human
escalation is a conditional branch at any phase, not an obligatory epic terminus.

Keep evidence and independent-review gates. Configured or explicitly reserved
human checkpoints remain binding; agent judgment cannot manufacture approval,
expand permissions, or erase a prior decision. Make delegated epic acceptance
explicit, evidence-bound and distinct from recorded human acceptance. Preserve
legacy configurations and historical records without silently broadening authority.

## Consequences

This supersedes unconditional per-epic human-checkpoint prose in earlier decisions,
not their evidence requirements or external-action authority. Update the canonical
skills, onboarding, applicable mechanics, examples and visuals together. Verify
actual setup and BMad availability; no release, deployment or monitoring access
is implied by this product amendment.
